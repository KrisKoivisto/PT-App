Based on what you loved and liked. Things you didn't like push similar titles down.

1. Your free TMDB key

TMDB is the free movie database this app looks things up in. It needs a key once.

  1. Make a free account at themoviedb.org.
  2. Open Settings → API and request a key. Pick Developer, and for the purpose write something like "personal watch list".
  3. Copy the API Read Access Token (the long one) or the API Key, and paste it here.

Just want to look around first? Try it with sample data.

2. Your streaming services

Tick what you pay for. Those get a green ring on every card.

3. Put it on your home screen

iPhone: open this page in Safari, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
Android: in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen or Install app.

It then opens like an app. On iPhone this also keeps Safari from clearing your list if you go a while without opening it.

Backup

Your list lives on this phone only. Save a backup now and then, or to move it to another device.

Import from IMDb (optional)

On IMDb, open Your Ratings (or your Watchlist), tap the three dots, choose Export, and pick the file here.

Taste

Loved counts as 10, Liked as 8, Didn't like as 3.
