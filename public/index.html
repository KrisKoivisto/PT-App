What to Watch

1. Import from IMDb

IMDb has no personal API, so this uses their CSV export. On IMDb, open Your Ratings, click the three dots, and choose Export. Do the same on your Watchlist. Importing again replaces the old data.

2. Your streaming services

Netflix, Disney+ and the rest don't expose your account, so tick what you pay for.

3. Taste

Streaming availability data from JustWatch via TMDB. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. Not affiliated with IMDb.
